# 100rel and PRACK

Owner: JAR-8. RFC 3262. Reliability state stays on our dialog: the last RSeq we sent, the RSeq we have PRACKed, and the provisional we are retransmitting. Confirm the sipgo v1.7.3 types while implementing. `sip.PRACK` exists. `DialogClientSession` has `Do` and `TransactionRequest` for a request the dialog helpers do not build. Do not fork sipgo.

`Supported: 100rel` is on our requests and responses. We do not send `Require: 100rel`.

## We receive a reliable provisional

A 1xx other than 100 that carries `RSeq`, or that the peer marked reliable, is answered with PRACK. The PRACK's `RAck` carries that RSeq, the method (INVITE), and the INVITE CSeq. We send one PRACK per RSeq. A retransmission of the same provisional does not send a second PRACK. `183` with SDP still starts early media ([early-media.md](early-media.md)) whether or not it is reliable. When it is reliable, we PRACK and we play.

A PRACK we receive is matched to the RSeq we sent. A match gets `200`. A mismatch gets `481` and the provisional keeps retransmitting until it times out.

## We send a reliable provisional

`100 Trying` is not reliable. Any other 1xx we send on an INVITE that offered or required `100rel` carries `RSeq` and `Require: 100rel`, and we retransmit it until PRACK or the transaction times out.

The basic inbound path is `100` then `200` ([inbound-dialog.md](inbound-dialog.md)). That path has no reliable provisional. `Require: 100rel` on the INVITE is still accepted: a 200 with no 18x satisfies it. When we do send `180` or `183`, those follow the rules above.
